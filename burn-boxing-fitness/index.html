FAQ

Everything you need to know.

Do I need to be fit to start?

Not at all. We work with all fitness levels — from complete beginners to experienced athletes. Every session is tailored to you.

What's a free consultation?

It's a no-pressure chat about your goals, fitness history, and how we can help. We'll show you the studio and answer any questions.

How is this different from a regular gym?

We're a private studio — no crowds, no waiting for equipment. Every session is coached and personalised. You'll never follow a generic plan.

How often should I train?

Most clients train 2-3 times per week. We'll recommend a schedule based on your goals and lifestyle during your consultation.

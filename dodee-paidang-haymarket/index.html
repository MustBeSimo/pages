The Chinatown bowl house

Fast, loud, spicy, useful.

Dodee Paidang Haymarket sits at 9/37 Ultimo Road, opposite the Market City end of Haymarket. Public listings describe it as Thai cuisine with takeout, outdoor dining, brunch and family-friendly service.

The sharper hook: the tom yum noodle menu, spice levels, Thai iced tea, pandan drink, pork skewers, som tum and street-food plates that make the place feel more Bangkok backstreet than generic Sydney Thai.

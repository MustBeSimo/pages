01 — The Frame

The thing you recommended yesterday is different today

Wabi is living software. Somebody remixed it overnight. The same user opens it again tomorrow because it bent around them — not because a push caught them at the right second.

That is a different product shape from anything the recommender-systems literature is built for.

Video recsys optimizes watch-ratio over a frozen catalog. Wabi's unit of value is a return to a specific app on a different day. Catalog picking vs. catalog making. Session length vs. distinct-day binding.

"Serve me something" vs. "mine, and it's mine-er today than yesterday."

02 — What We Found Upstream

Five mechanisms that matter

Six diagnostics before the redesign. Each pressure-tested from its own angle. Five mechanisms genuinely matter.

Mechanism 1

I3 is the north star, not dwell

I3: at least two opens of the same app on two distinct calendar days within a rolling 14-day window.

Distinct-day beats raw session count. Five frustrated opens on a Tuesday is not taste — it's the opposite.

The 14-day window is a hyperparameter, not a schema choice: a config knob against raw per-open timestamps, retunable without migration.

This implies the I3 raw-timestamp contract: raw per-open timestamps must be persisted, never rolled up to daily counts on ingestion. If production is already rolling up, the taste-embedding refit loop needs redesign.

Mechanism 2

Binding is the unit of retention

A binding = (user, app, context) with ≥3 distinct-day opens in 14 days, at least one organic (not push-triggered).

Two bindings by day 14 is the activation threshold:

0 bindings → churn. 1 binding → dabbler. 2 bindings → habit.

Organic is non-negotiable. Three push-driven opens is push-dependent, not bound. Optimizing for it is the vanity-retention trap that collapses at D60.

Binding State Machine

Six states, mechanism-gated transitions

Seed
1 open in context. Pending second within 14d.
Pending
2 opens, 2 distinct days. Needs 3rd with ≥1 organic.
Bound
3+ distinct-day opens. The retention unit. Decays after 21d no-event.
Cooling
21d no event. Next organic open → rebound. 45d → dormant.
Dormant
45d no event. Recovery path available via Explore row.
Revoked
E4 hide. 90d hard exclusion. Never auto-recovers.
Mechanism 3

Gap-aware recommendation

When the ranker has nothing good to offer — thin candidate union, a search + refinement that found nothing, or a cross-user cluster of unmet intent — Wabi logs the gap as a first-class event.

No consumer recsys logs its own misses. No app store offers to fill them.

Where a gap matches a human-curated template from a ~20–50 library (reminder / tracker / scheduler / counter / journaling-prompt), Wabi offers a templated proposal: "Want us to make a vet-scheduler for you?"

Named template, named capability. No mystery-box. Open-ended LLM synthesis is v2.

Mechanism 4

Quality is a floor, not an average

Two numbers, composing multiplicatively:

Q_a — quality, user-independent. Seven-dimension rubric scored against per-category golden samples (App Store Today / Michelin / Homebrew lineage).

<T_u, C_a> — taste fit, user-specific. Matrix factorization over I3 cells.

M(a, m) — moment fit, context-specific.

Crucial rule: high-taste × low-quality apps are demoted, not shipped. A taste-matched bad app is the worst recsys outcome — user trusted us, we wasted their time.

Q_a ≥ 0.35 enforced at candidate generation. The ranker never sees sub-floor apps. Anti-npm stance.

Mechanism 5

Engagement-state biases retriever mix

engagement_state ∈ {active, cooling, at-risk, recovered, churned} is read at candidate generation and shifts quota, not retriever logic.

At-risk users get a binding-recovery retriever pulling apps they had I3 with 60d ago, replacing ~30% of taste-match slots.

This composes with a presentation-layer row-order bias under a compositional cap: ≤40% recovery impressions, ≥15% discovery floor.

Without that cap, two layers of "we know you're in trouble" bias stack into pure nostalgia at precisely the moment a fresh hook would help most.

03 — Explore Tab V1

Two surfaces, one ranker core

Mobile Explore — scrollable cards, N~20, 15% exploration floor.

Desktop widget glance — 1 or 3 slots, 5% floor, daily-cohort rotation.

Both consume the same surface_recs API. Same T_u. Same Q_a ≥ 0.35 floor. Same impression-logging contract.

Mobile optimizes for I3. Widget optimizes for W8 — the widget→mobile open with referrer=widget.

Auto-halt guards: I3 drop >10% → halt. Sessions drop >15% → halt. E4 increase >50% → halt. Push ignore >30% → halt. Recovery cap breach → halt.

Methodology: shadow the new ranker for 14d (counterfactual, no user risk). A/B at 10% → 50% gated on primary metrics. Min 2000 users/arm.

From You

Three load-bearing questions

Creator identity model. Anonymous (a) vs. handle-based (b, our default) vs. verified-identity (c). This decides M13 retention, attribution copy, npm exposure, and v2 trust moat. Build-time feature-flagged; flag must be chosen before launch copy freezes.

A note from the principal

Slow law, in a fast town.

Forty years ago I opened a small practice on Lower Plenty Road because I believed legal advice should sound like a conversation, not an invoice line item. That belief hasn't aged. If anything, it has become the rarer thing.

Our work is unglamorous on purpose: a contract read carefully, a will written plainly, a phone call returned the same day. We say no to matters we cannot do well, and we explain why. We charge fixed fees so the meter never runs faster than the thinking.

You won't get junior staff and a spreadsheet at Belvedere Lawyers. You'll get one of us — usually three of us — sitting across a table or a phone line, until your matter is closed and your file is quiet.

Common questions

Before you call, the basics.

Yes. For standard residential conveyancing in Victoria we quote a single fixed fee covering the full transaction — including Section 32 review, contract advice and settlement attendance. Disbursements (PEXA, searches, transfer duty) are passed through at cost.

Most clients do at least one short meeting — by phone, video or in our Rosanna office — so we understand your circumstances. Drafting and review can then happen by email, and signing is done in person to satisfy witnessing requirements.

For genuinely time-critical matters — a settlement at risk, an offer about to lapse, an urgent power of attorney — we will usually arrange a same-day call and start work immediately. Just say "urgent" when you call or write.

Most of our clients are in the north-eastern Melbourne suburbs, but we act on Victorian property and estate matters statewide. For interstate matters we'll happily refer you to a trusted firm in the relevant jurisdiction.

An initial call to understand whether we're the right fit is always complimentary. If your matter requires substantive advice on the spot, we'll let you know up front before any chargeable work begins.

Our Story

Built by mums, for mums

Fitmum_abs was born from a simple belief: mothers deserve a fitness space that truly understands them. No crowded gyms, no intimidating equipment, no guilt.

Our Fraser Rise studio is a judgement-free zone where you can focus on your wellbeing while building genuine connections with other local mums who just get it.

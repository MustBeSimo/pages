Proactive App Seeding Pilot
A 3-week activation experiment to reduce Wabi's education gap through direct utility.

THE PROBLEM
People do not understand what mini apps are quickly enough to experience their value. The current model
asks users to arrive, understand the concept, imagine what they could build, and then create. That is too much
cognitive work before utility. Most users drop before first value.
This pilot tests a simpler path: deliver a useful app first, let usage teach the format, and measure whether
activation improves.

THE HYPOTHESIS
Seeded mini apps delivered directly to target users will increase activation (measured as
core-action completion within 24 hours of link open) by at least 2x vs baseline organic
acquisition, and produce measurable early retention signal at D7.
Falsifiable: if seeded users do not activate at 2x baseline within the pilot window, the mechanic does not work
at current scale.
Framed in AARRR terms, this is an activation experiment with early retention signal, not a retention project.
D30 retention will be instrumented now and read after the pilot window closes.
I have tested this mechanic in another vertical (identifying targets with an unmet need, building the solution
before asking, distributing directly) with early positive signal. This pilot applies the same logic to Wabi's context
and measures the result.

WHERE THIS GOES
YouTube was built pre-AI. A video never improves after upload. Wabi exists in a different reality: mini apps are
software, and software can be living. A seeded app is not a static delivery, it is the entry point into an evolving
relationship.
The AI-native retention loop:
Seed -> Use -> AI observes -> App proactively adapts -> The magic moment (improvement without
asking) -> Next app generated before asked -> Personal software ecosystem compounds.
This pilot tests stage one of that loop. The rest becomes relevant only if stage one works.

PILOT SCOPE
3 weeks. 10-12 apps across 3-5 segments. Focused on activation. Retention observed as secondary signal,
not guaranteed.

Week 1: Segment validation and app design
- Validate 3-5 target segments with clear triggers and jobs-to-be-done
- Define core action and first-value criteria per app
- Draft event taxonomy and measurement plan

Week 2: Build, QA, and instrument
- Build 10-12 mini apps with a strict quality bar
- Instrument core events and generate tracked distribution links
- Prepare controlled comparison (seeded vs non-seeded or channel holdout)

Week 3: Distribute, measure, and report
- Ship distribution across segment-specific channels
- Track activation, time-to-first-value, D1 and D7 signal, remix rate
- Deliver results report with scale recommendation

DELIVERABLES
- 10-12 mini apps live on Wabi, mapped to specific segments
- Segment-to-app mapping framework (reusable for future seeding)
- Distribution playbook with tracked links and channel analysis
- Results report: activation data, early retention signal, next-step recommendations
